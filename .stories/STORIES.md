## Sale browsing

### US-001: See what's coming
~ As a shopper, I want a list of sales that have not started yet, ordered by when they open, so that I can plan a purchase before the discount goes live.
**Epic / Parent:** Sale browsing

### US-002: Narrow to one status
~ As a shopper, I want to filter the list to upcoming, live, or ended sales, so that I can separate what I can act on now from what I am waiting for.
**Epic / Parent:** Sale browsing

### US-003: Know exactly when it opens
~ As a shopper, I want each sale to show its start time in my own timezone and how long until it opens, so that I do not have to convert times or guess.
**Epic / Parent:** Sale browsing

### US-004: Share what I am looking at
~ As a shopper, I want the filtered view to be a link I can send or bookmark, so that a friend opens the same list I am seeing.
**Epic / Parent:** Sale browsing

## Brand pages

### US-005: See one brand's sales
~ As a shopper, I want a page per brand listing that brand's upcoming and current sales, so that I can check a single brand without wading through everything else.
**Epic / Parent:** Brand pages

### US-006: Find a brand
~ As a shopper, I want to search or browse an index of covered brands, so that I can tell whether the brand I care about is tracked here at all.
**Epic / Parent:** Brand pages

## Following

### US-007: Follow a brand
~ As a shopper, I want to mark a brand as followed, so that I do not re-find it every visit.
**Epic / Parent:** Following

### US-008: See only who I follow
~ As a shopper, I want a view limited to sales from brands I follow, so that the list reflects the handful of brands I actually buy from.
**Epic / Parent:** Following
